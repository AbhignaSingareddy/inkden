FAQ

Before we begin.

A few answers to common questions about starting an InkDen project.

How does the first conversation work?

We start by understanding the room, your collection, how you use the space and the atmosphere you want to create. From there, we can discuss possible directions and next steps.

Do I need room measurements before contacting you?

No. Measurements are helpful when available, but they are not required for the first conversation.

Can you work with an existing room?

Yes. Existing furniture, architecture, lighting and finishes can all be considered while developing the library or reading space.

Do you design custom shelving?

Yes. Custom shelving can be developed around your collection, available wall dimensions, storage needs and overall design direction.

How do I get started?

Simply send us a message through the contact form or use the Enquire button. Tell us a little about your space and we will take it from there.
